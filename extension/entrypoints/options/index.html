      Settings

      Gemini API key

      Free, about a minute, no card needed: open Google AI Studio, sign in with your Google account, press Create API key, copy it and paste it below.

      Get a free key

      The key stays in this browser. It is used to read your resume and to answer questions your profile does not cover.

      Account password (optional)

      Workday makes you create an account for every employer. Save one password here and the extension creates the account (or signs in) with your profile's email and this password, and agrees to the account terms for you. It is stored unencrypted in this browser only. Use a password made for job applications, not one you use anywhere else.

      All sites

      Employers with their own careers site (Meta, Amazon, Microsoft) are not on the built-in list. Allow all sites and the Auto-fill banner shows by itself on their application forms too; without it, click the toolbar button on the form.

      Job loop

      Needs All sites above. On a job board, click the toolbar button and press Loop jobs: each listed job opens in its own tab (or inside the board, where the board applies in place), is auto-filled, and the loop returns to the board for the next, scrolling for more jobs as the list runs out. It pauses after 25 jobs.

      Used wherever an application asks you to sign in or create an account first. Stored unencrypted in this browser and sent only to the site that asks: pick a password you use nowhere else.

      Off: the loop fills and leaves every tab for you. On: it also presses Next and Submit, but only on a step where nothing is left highlighted for you. Gemini's answers and essay drafts are sent as written, unread.
